TRACK 14 · BREAKING THE PATTERN

Started This War

Featuring Nick “Nasty” Addezio, the song turns conflict into a question about when enduring a pattern becomes participating in it.

The HLR connection

Nick is tied directly to Mark Allen’s return, collaborator reconnections and the A&R structure of Hustle Legend Records. His appearance therefore belongs to the album's real-world story, not just the feature list.

The song arrives immediately after Rowdy, so independence is followed by a record about what happens when boundaries finally get enforced.

Verified lyric annotation

“You don't see the cage when the bars feel warm”

“Bars” carries two meanings at once: the bars of a cage and rap bars. The image suggests a harmful situation can feel normal, familiar or even comfortable enough that the confinement becomes hard to recognize.

Nick NastyConflictBoundariesDouble meaning

The bigger B2BAL idea

Across the album, freedom is emotional, creative and structural. This line compresses that theme into one image: not every cage looks hostile from the inside.
