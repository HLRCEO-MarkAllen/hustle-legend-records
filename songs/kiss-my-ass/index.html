TRACK 11 · FULL-CIRCLE FEATURE

Kiss My Ass

Featuring Ill Will, this is one of the clearest places where the 2026 album folds back into the origin of Mark Allen’s rap story.

Why the feature matters

Ill Will is tied to Mark’s early inspiration as a rapper. That makes the feature more than a guest appearance: somebody connected to the beginning of the journey appears inside the comeback album itself.

That full-circle element fits B2BAL’s larger obsession with time, return and proving that old chapters can reappear with a completely different meaning years later.

Album function

After King Cobra, the defiance continues—but the history underneath this track makes it feel less isolated and more generational.

Ill WillFull circleDefianceHistory

What to hear differently

Listen to it not only as a confrontational record, but as evidence that the B2BAL era was reconnecting pieces of Mark’s music life that had once seemed separated by years.
