TRACK 06 · NO CENSOR BUTTON

Bite My Tongue

A pressure-release record built around one of the album’s defining ideas: what happens when an artist decides self-censorship is no longer part of the deal.

What the song is doing

Bite My Tongue turns restraint itself into the enemy. In the larger B2BAL story, the song sits after records dealing with identity, family and memory, then snaps the camera back toward confrontation.

The point is not simply shock value. The song represents Mark Allen’s belief that rap works best when the writer is willing to say the uncomfortable thing, accept the reaction and stand behind the record afterward.

Why it matters on the album

It helps explain the tone of everything that follows. Once the album establishes that Mark is not editing himself for approval, the later battle records, social commentary and darker material make more sense.

DefianceFree expressionConflictIdentity

Listen for

Listen for the way aggression functions as character development. The performance is not separate from the autobiography; it is part of the autobiography. B2BAL keeps asking who gets to define Mark Allen, and this song answers: not the people asking him to tone himself down.
