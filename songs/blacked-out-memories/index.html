05 · ANESTHESIA

Blacked Out Memories

A darker chapter where drinking stops sounding like celebration and starts sounding like an attempt not to feel.

Behind the song

The track connects memory, environment and alcohol. Forest Creek is part of that personal geography, but the larger idea is broader: sometimes substances become less about getting high or drunk and more about trying to turn the volume down on yourself.

Why it matters

The album repeatedly returns to the question of what people do with pain when they do not yet know how to process it. Here, forgetting becomes both the goal and the consequence. The title captures the contradiction perfectly: the moments are gone, but the damage still remembers.
