Told U

Featuring Torown, “Told U” represents one of the comeback album's full-circle collaborations.

Behind the song

The connection with Torown began years before Born 2 Be A Legend, when a feature was discussed but life interrupted the plan. After Mark Allen returned to music and Hustle Legend Records took shape, the collaboration finally happened. The result became proof that some creative ideas survive the years between them.

Released April 5, 2026 on Born 2 Be A Legend.
