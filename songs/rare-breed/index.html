03 · SURVIVOR IDENTITY

Rare Breed

Survival stops being a wound and becomes evidence.

The role of the song

After the return and the personal fallout of the opening tracks, “Rare Breed” begins rebuilding identity. Instead of letting hardship define the artist only through damage, the record flips the frame: surviving the damage is itself part of the definition.

Why it matters

The title is a self-description, but not just a flex. In the album's larger story, it marks the point where Mark starts naming himself instead of letting outside labels do it for him. That idea eventually pays off again in “Who I Am.”
