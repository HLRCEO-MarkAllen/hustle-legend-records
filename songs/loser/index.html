09 · THE LABEL THEY GIVE YOU

Loser

A bruised word becomes a test: do other people's judgments get to become your identity?

The idea

“Loser” sits at the intersection of insecurity and defiance. The title sounds like a verdict, but the song treats it more like an accusation to push against. That tension fits the album's larger obsession with who gets to define Mark Allen—other people, old circumstances, or the artist himself.

Why it matters

Placed before “King Cobra,” the track helps turn self-doubt outward into confrontation. It also sets up the later resolution of “Who I Am,” where the album returns to the identity question with more control and less need for outside approval.
