Born 2 Be A Legend · Track Story

S.O.S.

A warning-shot record from the darker side of Born 2 Be A Legend, built around distrust, power, corruption, and the cost of speaking on things people would rather ignore.

Behind the song

Placed after “Truth Bomb,” “S.O.S.” pushes the album deeper into its confrontational lane. The track expands the project's themes of censorship, institutional power, and suspicion of official narratives while keeping the focus on Mark Allen's personal point of view rather than presenting speculation as fact.

Released April 5, 2026 on Born 2 Be A Legend.
