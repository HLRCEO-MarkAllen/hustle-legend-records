Image Consultant · Creative Collaborator

VISUAL IDENTITY · STORY · CREATIVE DIRECTION

A creative partnership beyond the cover.

Danielle “Barb!3” Hein helps shape how Hustle Legend Records looks, communicates and presents its story. Her role connects visual identity, story development, design, promotion and reputation guidance.

A long-term creative collaborator of Mark Allen, Danielle brings continuity to the label’s presentation and creative development. During the Born 2 Be A Legend era, her contributions extend from the image around the music to writing credits within the album itself.
