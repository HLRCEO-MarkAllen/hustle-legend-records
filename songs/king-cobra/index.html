10 · BATTLE RAP AS BOUNDARY · LEGEND FILE 010

King Cobra

The insults are the fireworks. Underneath them is an argument about ownership, respect and who gets to define the artist.

Conflict is the surface. Self-definition is the spine.

On an autobiographical album, confrontation matters most when it reveals what the narrator refuses to surrender.

Three functions

BATTLEThe record uses direct confrontation and competitive rap energy.
BOUNDARYThe aggression draws a line around respect, work and artistic value.
ALBUMAfter “Loser” questions outside judgment, “King Cobra” answers that pressure directly.

Why it belongs in the story

Born 2 Be A Legend does not clean up every ugly emotion. This track leaves conflict visible because the record is built as autobiography rather than reputation management.

The deeper tension is value: whether somebody outside the artist gets to diminish the work, history or person behind it. That keeps the focus on Mark Allen's story rather than turning the HLR site into promotion for an opponent.
