04 · CHOSEN FAMILY

Hood Fam

Featuring Alina, the song treats loyalty as something you can build when home has not always felt permanent.

The idea

“Hood Fam” turns instability into a broader community story. Foster homes, group-home memories, distrust and Forest Creek all feed the idea that family can be chosen through survival and loyalty as much as blood.

Verified lyric annotations

“Foster home to group spot, slept with the lights on / Trust was a myth, survival my theme song”

The first half is literal environment. The second turns survival into music itself: if survival is the “theme song,” then the life story has been scoring the album long before the album existed.

“Forest creek parties where I masked my business”

This is one of the cleanest keys to the Forest Creek chapter. The parties are the public surface; “masked my business” admits that pain and instability were being concealed underneath the social chaos.

Why it matters

The chosen-family idea also foreshadows Hustle Legend Records. The B2BAL era is full of people who stayed connected, returned and helped turn a comeback into a team.
