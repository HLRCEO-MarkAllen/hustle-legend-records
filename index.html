WHY THIS ALBUM EXISTS

Not a comeback stunt. A record of survival.

Born 2 Be A Legend is the sound of Mark Allen returning after a five-year interruption and refusing to erase the missing time. The album moves like memory: not in a straight line, but through flashbacks, wounds, jokes, battle bars, family, addiction, identity, chosen family, ambition and recovery.

The bars often carry two lives at once. A punchline can land as a joke or flex first, then reveal a much heavier memory underneath. “Rock bottom” becomes architecture. “Scars” become footnotes. Owning a track becomes a metaphor for owning the right to tell your own story.

“The title is not proof that the legend already happened. It is the decision to survive long enough to build one.”

ConflictKing Cobra and Started This War make boundaries loud enough to hear.
OwnershipRowdy and Dreams connect creative freedom to rights, masters and the label itself.
IdentityLoser and Who I Am ask who gets to decide what a person is worth.
LegacyThe whole project treats survival, documentation and ownership as parts of the same future.
