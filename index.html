Born 2 Be A Legend

Born 2 Be A Legend is the sound of Mark Allen taking authorship back. The record moves the way memory moves: not in a clean straight line, but through flashbacks, wounds, jokes, battle bars, family, addiction, identity, betrayal, chosen family, ambition and recovery.

Its strongest trick is that the punchlines often carry two lives at once. A bar can hit as a joke, a flex or a battle-rap shot on first listen, then reveal a much heavier memory underneath. “Rock bottom” becomes architecture. “Scars” become footnotes. Owning a track becomes a metaphor for owning the right to tell your own story.

The album begins with resurrection in 5 Years Later..., moves through relationship fallout, alcoholism, belonging, conflict and self-definition, then closes by widening the camera toward belief, institutions and the darker questions sitting behind the personal story. The title is not presented as fame already achieved. It is a decision to survive long enough to become the narrator.

THE LYRIC & STORY MAP

20 Tracks. One Life Told Sideways.

The album is built like memory: scenes return out of order, names and places become symbols, and punchlines act like trapdoors into older chapters.

01 · THE RESURRECTION
5 Years Later...

The missing years become first-person testimony. The wordplay turns “rock bottom” into a place with another floor beneath it — an image for discovering that even your lowest point can still collapse further.

“Rock bottom had a basement.”
02 · CONSEQUENCE
Karma

A relationship record where breakup language collides with fatherhood and consequence. The opening voice of Mark's daughter makes the emotional stakes bigger than romance: separation has witnesses.

03 · SURVIVOR IDENTITY
Rare Breed

Instead of defining himself by what almost destroyed him, Mark defines himself as the exception that lived long enough to speak. Survival becomes evidence.

04 · CHOSEN FAMILY
Hood Fam feat. Alina

After years when “home” kept changing addresses, family becomes something built through loyalty as much as blood.

05 · ANESTHESIA
Blacked Out Memories

Drinking stops sounding celebratory and starts sounding medicinal. Forest Creek becomes a location in the story, and alcohol is framed less as a party than an attempt to numb identity and memory.

06 · NO CENSOR BUTTON
Bite My Tongue

The album's refusal to self-sanitize goes nuclear here. It connects to the artistic lesson Mark took from fearless rappers: say what people are scared to say, then own the reaction.

07 · CONFRONTATION
Got a Problem feat. Scumbag Rick

Feature chemistry turns tension into performance. In the larger arc, confrontation becomes another way of refusing to be minimized.

08 · MEMORY LOOP
Deja Vu

The title fits the album's flashback structure: old patterns feel new until the listener recognizes the repetition.

09 · THE LABEL THEY GIVE YOU
Loser

A bruised title becomes a challenge: whether somebody else's judgment gets to become identity. That tension sets up the later self-definition of Who I Am.

10 · BATTLE RAP AS BOUNDARY
King Cobra

The insults are the fireworks; underneath them is an ownership argument. The real conflict is over who gets to define the artist or assign value to his work.

11 · FULL-CIRCLE FEATURE
Kiss My Ass feat. Ill Will

Defiance meets history. Ill Will is tied to Mark's early inspiration, so the feature folds the present-day album back toward the beginning of the rap journey.

12 · ENVIRONMENT
All Alone

The camera pulls back from one person's pain to the places that manufacture pain. “Scars as footnotes” turns damage into documentation.

“Scars read like footnotes.”
13 · OWNERSHIP AS PUNCHLINE
Rowdy

Independence stops being business jargon and becomes swagger. “Can't be shelved” means both an artist who cannot be benched by a label and a person refusing to be put away by somebody else's decision.

“Can't be shelved if I own my tracks.”
14 · BREAKING THE PATTERN
Started This War feat. Nick Nasty

A collaboration with HLR's A&R that frames conflict as what happens when abusive or corrupt patterns are finally challenged.

15 · CONNECTION COMPLETED
Told U feat. Torown

A long-discussed collaboration finally becomes part of the official catalog, closing a loop that began years before the album existed.

16 · ARMOR OFF
My Angel

Placed late in an album full of conflict, this record changes the temperature. Gratitude and devotion create breathing room before the final run.

17 · FUTURE OWNERSHIP
Dreams

Dreaming is no longer passive. The future belongs to the artist who learned the paperwork: masters, publishing, credits and rights.

18 · SELF-DEFINITION
Who I Am

Aliases, criticism and labels get stripped away. Even ADHD becomes part of the argument: a diagnosis can describe a trait without being allowed to fully define the person.

19 · THE CAMERA TURNS OUTWARD
Truth Bomb

The album moves beyond autobiography into institutions, corruption, censorship and uncomfortable questions. Personal distrust becomes social commentary.

20 · DARKEST CONCEPTUAL CHAPTER
S.O.S.

Following Truth Bomb, the closer pushes the record into its most confrontational conceptual territory and ends without pretending every question has been resolved.

ARTIST BIO

Mark Allen

Mark Allen is an independent hip-hop artist, songwriter, producer, engineer and founder/CEO of Hustle Legend Records. His music history reaches back to the early 2000s, including the Prodigal Son era, the 2004 mixtape History In The Making, later projects such as Intoxicated, Poetic Therapy, Sobriety and Are You Listening?, and a return to releasing after a five-year interruption.

His writing style is built around punchlines with a second floor underneath them: similes, compact rhyme schemes, multi-meaning phrases, dark humor, confession and battle-rap aggression. Influences including 2Pac, Eminem, King Crooked, Lloyd Banks and Papoose helped shape a philosophy that values fearless expression and technical wordplay without separating the bars from lived experience.

By the time Born 2 Be A Legend arrived in 2026, Mark was not only thinking like an artist. He was thinking about masters, publishing, registrations, credits, ISRCs, UPCs and long-term catalog ownership. Hustle Legend Records became the business structure around that mindset: create the art, understand the paperwork, keep the story authentic and build something that survives longer than a release-week algorithm.

The legend was never the guy who never fell. It was the kid who survived long enough to tell you where the scars came from.
