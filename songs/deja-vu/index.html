08 · MEMORY LOOP · LEGEND FILE 008

Deja Vu

Repeated loss becomes structure: every new goodbye can wake up an older one.

THE TITLE MECHANIC

THE PRESENT FEELS FAMILIAR
BECAUSE THE WOUND ISN'T NEW.

The song uses déjà vu as emotional pattern recognition rather than a supernatural event.

Inside the Bars

VERIFIED LINE 01
“same time you passed I died inside”

Literal death is paired with an internal one. The loss doesn't only remove somebody; it changes the narrator who remains.

VERIFIED LINE 02
“cause losing people that I love feels like Deja Vu”

The title resolves into its thesis: new grief arrives carrying the emotional shape of older grief.

Catch it?

Why does the second line make the song bigger than one specific loss?

Because the line turns a single event into a repeating pattern. “People” is plural and “déjà vu” describes recurrence. The song can therefore hold multiple losses inside one emotional mechanism.

Why Track 08 matters

Born 2 Be A Legend doesn't move like a perfectly straight autobiography. Memories interrupt the present. Old consequences reappear inside new situations. Deja Vu gives that storytelling behavior a name.

That makes memory itself part of the album's architecture—not just something the lyrics happen to mention.
