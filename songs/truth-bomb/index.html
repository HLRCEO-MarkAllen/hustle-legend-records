Truth Bomb

A confrontational record about distrust, censorship, corruption, and the instinct to question stories that are presented as settled fact.

Behind the song

“Truth Bomb” is one of the album's most outspoken records. Its role is less about proving every suspicion and more about documenting Mark Allen's mindset: skeptical of powerful institutions, frustrated by censorship, and unwilling to keep controversial questions out of his music simply because they make people uncomfortable.

Released April 5, 2026 on Born 2 Be A Legend.
