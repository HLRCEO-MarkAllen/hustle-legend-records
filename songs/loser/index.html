Born 2 Be A Legend · Track Story

Loser

A track about what happens when somebody else's judgment becomes a label — and what it takes to stop wearing it.

Behind the song

“Loser” turns rejection and disrespect into fuel. Instead of accepting the version of himself projected by other people, Mark Allen flips the insult into a statement about survival, identity, and self-definition. It fits the larger album theme of refusing to wait for outside approval.

Released April 5, 2026 on Born 2 Be A Legend.
