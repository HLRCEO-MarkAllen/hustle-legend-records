TRACK 08 · MEMORY LOOP

Deja Vu

A song built around repetition: the unsettling feeling that a present moment is really an old pattern wearing new clothes.

Why the title fits the album

B2BAL is structured through memory rather than a perfectly straight timeline. Deja Vu makes that structure explicit. Old emotions, behaviors and conflicts can return so convincingly that the listener has to ask whether anything actually changed.

The title also works as a warning: recognizing a pattern does not automatically mean you have escaped it.

Visual language

The track’s visual concepts have leaned toward cracked reflections, timepieces and dark urban imagery—symbols that fit a song about seeing yourself inside something that feels familiar.

MemoryPatternsReflectionTime

Place in the sequence

After the confrontational energy of Track 7, Deja Vu bends inward again. It keeps the album from becoming one-note and reinforces the idea that external conflict and internal memory are constantly feeding each other.
