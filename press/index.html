STORY ANGLES

What makes the current era worth covering.

The five-year return

The album opens with “5 Years Later…,” framing a comeback after a long interruption and making the gap itself part of the story rather than hiding it.

One artist building a label

Mark Allen is not only releasing records; he is building Hustle Legend Records around owned masters, publishing, direct-to-fan infrastructure, documented history and long-term catalog value.

Twenty tracks as one narrative

The album is organized as a connected body of work, with song pages, a timeline, lyrics, reactions and an interactive story map that let listeners move deeper into the same era.
