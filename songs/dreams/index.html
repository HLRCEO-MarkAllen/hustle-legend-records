TRACK 17 · FUTURE OWNERSHIP

Dreams

A future-facing chapter where ambition is no longer just fantasy; it is tied to ownership, paperwork, catalog building and the decision to make the dream structurally real.

Dreaming with receipts

By this point in the album, Mark Allen’s idea of success is not limited to being heard. The B2BAL era is also about learning masters, publishing, credits, registrations, ISRCs and the mechanics of keeping control of the work.

That gives Dreams a practical edge: ambition becomes something you can build systems around.

Why it belongs late

After survival and conflict, the album finally has room to look forward. The future sounds different when the narrator believes he owns the road underneath it.

AmbitionOwnershipLegacyFuture

The B2BAL connection

The title of the album is a promise about what comes next. Dreams is where that promise starts sounding less like fantasy and more like a plan.
