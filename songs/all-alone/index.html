All Alone

A darker chapter that widens the lens from personal pain to the environments that shape it.

From feeling to evidence

All Alone sits in the album as more than isolation for isolation’s sake. The record connects internal damage to the places and circumstances that help create it.

That is why the idea of scars reading like footnotes fits so well: damage becomes documentation. The body and memory both keep receipts.

Album role

Track 12 helps bridge the conflict-heavy middle section into the ownership run that follows with Rowdy.

Verified bar annotation

“Scars read like footnotes.”

Surface: scars are visible marks left by something painful.

Wordplay: footnotes explain the source behind a statement. Calling scars “footnotes” turns physical or emotional damage into citations—proof that the story has receipts attached to it.

Album connection: Born 2 Be A Legend repeatedly refuses to present survival as a clean victory. The evidence of what happened remains on the narrator, and that evidence becomes part of the storytelling.
