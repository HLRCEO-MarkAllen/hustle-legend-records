Born 2 Be A Legend

Born 2 Be A Legend is the sound of Mark Allen returning after a five-year interruption and refusing to come back quietly. The record moves like memory moves: not in a straight line, but through flashbacks, wounds, jokes, battle bars, family, addiction, identity, betrayal, chosen family, ambition and recovery.

The punchlines often carry two lives at once. A bar can land as a joke, flex or battle-rap shot on the first listen, then reveal a heavier memory underneath. “Rock bottom” becomes architecture. “Scars” become footnotes. Owning a track becomes a metaphor for owning the right to tell your own story.

The album begins with resurrection in 5 Years Later..., moves through consequence, belonging, conflict and self-definition, then widens the camera toward belief, institutions and the darker questions behind the personal story.

The title is not a claim that the legend already happened. It is the decision to survive long enough to build one.

B2BAL ERA ARTIST BIO

Mark Allen

Mark Allen is an independent hip-hop artist, songwriter, producer, engineer and founder/CEO of Hustle Legend Records. Born 2 Be A Legend marks the defining chapter of his return to releasing music after a five-year interruption — not as an artist waiting to be discovered, but as one building the structure around his own catalog.

His writing on the album leans on layered punchlines, compact schemes, dark humor, confession and battle-rap aggression. The recurring trick is that a line can work as entertainment first and autobiography second: the joke hits, then the scar underneath it becomes visible.

By the April 5, 2026 release, Mark was thinking simultaneously about records and infrastructure: masters, publishing, registrations, credits, ISRCs, UPCs and long-term ownership. Hustle Legend Records became the business identity around that mindset — create the art, understand the paperwork, own the vision and document the chapter properly.

By September 2026, Mark reported approximately 12.2K monthly Spotify listeners, giving the Born 2 Be A Legend era a measurable growth story alongside its personal one.

The legend is not the guy who never fell. It is the one who survived long enough to tell you where the scars came from.
