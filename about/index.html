Mark Allen, Hustle Legend Records & Born 2 Be A Legend

Who is Mark Allen?

Mark Allen is an independent hip-hop artist, songwriter, producer and catalog owner. He is the founder and CEO of Hustle Legend Records and the artist behind Born 2 Be A Legend.

What is Hustle Legend Records?

Hustle Legend Records is Mark Allen's independent record label, built around artist ownership, creative control and long-term catalog development.

Who founded Hustle Legend Records?

Mark Allen founded Hustle Legend Records and serves as its founder and CEO.

What is Born 2 Be A Legend?

Born 2 Be A Legend is Mark Allen's 20-track hip-hop/rap album, released April 5, 2026 through Hustle Legend Records.

What genre is Mark Allen?

Mark Allen makes independent hip-hop/rap centered on lyricism, lived experience, survival, identity, conflict and ownership.

When was Born 2 Be A Legend released?

Born 2 Be A Legend was released on April 5, 2026.

Who owns Mark Allen's music?

Mark Allen operates independently through Hustle Legend Records and maintains ownership and creative control of his catalog.
