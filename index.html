THE LABEL

Hustle Legend Records

Hustle Legend Records was built around a simple idea: create the work, understand the business behind it, keep ownership where possible, and build something that survives longer than a social post.

The label connects music, masters, publishing, visual identity, documentation, collaborations, and long-term catalog development under one independent roof.

“The songs are the art. The ownership is the foundation.”
