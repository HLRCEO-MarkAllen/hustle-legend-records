WHAT HLR CONTROLS

Build the station without renting the identity.

ProgrammingHLR decides the queue, priority records, shows, themes and discovery lanes.
Front EndThe station lives on hustlelegendrecords.com instead of being reduced to a third-party profile.
Catalog RoutingEvery programmed record can route listeners into HLR song pages, merch, licensing and the larger ecosystem.
Next LayerDirect web audio, artist submissions, recently played history, DJ shows and the installable HLR app can attach to this same station.
Rights first: HLR Radio will only directly transmit audio that HLR has the right to stream in this use. Public radio-style transmission of third-party recordings can trigger licensing obligations even when the software infrastructure itself costs $0.
