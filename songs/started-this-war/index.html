TRACK 14 · BREAKING THE PATTERN

Started This War

Featuring Nick “Nasty” Addezio, this track brings one of the people at the center of the Hustle Legend Records story directly into the album.

The HLR connection

Nick is not simply a feature name in the credits. He is tied to Mark Allen’s return, the reconnection with collaborators, and the A&R role inside Hustle Legend Records. That makes his appearance part of the album’s real-world infrastructure.

The track frames conflict around the point where tolerating a pattern ends and answering it begins.

Why Track 14 matters

It arrives immediately after Rowdy, so the album’s independence statement is followed by a record involving the team helping turn that independence into an actual label structure.

Nick NastyHLRConflictLoyalty

The bigger B2BAL idea

The album repeatedly asks what happens when somebody stops accepting the role other people assigned them. Started This War turns that question into confrontation, with the HLR partnership sitting inside the record rather than outside it.
