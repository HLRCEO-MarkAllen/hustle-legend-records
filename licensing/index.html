DELIVERY

Built for real clearance conversations.

For suitable cleared tracks, Hustle Legend Records can provide high-resolution WAV masters, clean versions, instrumentals, a cappellas, tracked-out stems, lyrics and release metadata. Availability is confirmed track by track before any license is finalized.

Rights first

HLR verifies the master, publishing, featured-performer and other relevant rights for the specific recording under consideration. A song is not represented as one-stop unless the required rights are actually controlled or cleared for that use.

Useful alternates

Supervisors can ask about clean, instrumental, a cappella and stem-based versions when those assets are available for the selected track.

Fast metadata

Requests can include title, artist, writers, publishers, identifiers, ownership notes, lyrics and contact information so clearance does not begin with a scavenger hunt.

Clearance note: availability depends on the specific track, featured performers, intended use, territory, term and other rights considerations. This page invites inquiries; it does not promise one-click clearance.

Tell us what you're making.

Include the track, project type, intended media, territory, term, budget, deadline and whether you need an alternate version. HLR will confirm what can actually be cleared before terms are accepted.
