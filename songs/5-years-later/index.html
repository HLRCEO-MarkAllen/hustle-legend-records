Born 2 Be A Legend · Opening Track

5 Years Later…

The comeback begins here. After a five-year interruption, Mark Allen opens the album by confronting the distance between who he was, what disappeared, and why he came back.

Behind the song

“5 Years Later…” works like the first scene of a movie. It does not pretend the missing years never happened; it makes them part of the story. The track establishes the central tension of Born 2 Be A Legend: losing time, losing pieces of a catalog and identity, then rebuilding with more ownership and purpose than before.

Released April 5, 2026 on Born 2 Be A Legend.
