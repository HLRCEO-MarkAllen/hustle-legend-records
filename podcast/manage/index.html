03 · OWN YOUR SALES

Independent podcast store

Owner keeps the proceeds after payment and fulfillment costs. HLR takes no hosting cut.

  1. Use the host's own PayPal Business account.
  2. Create a PayPal payment link for each approved product with its actual price, currency and fulfillment details.
  3. Paste that product's checkout link below. Confirm that checkout shows the correct seller before release.
  4. HLR reviews the store owner, products, customer contact and policies before publication.

Entering an email does not connect PayPal. This desk accepts PayPal-hosted checkout links; it does not connect accounts or split a shared cart. Physical merch fulfillment must be arranged separately.
