THE COMPANY

Built because the catalog needed a permanent home.

Hustle Legend Records was founded by Mark Allen to connect music, ownership, artist development and business infrastructure under one independent system. The current flagship is Allen’s 20-track Born 2 Be A Legend, but HLR is being built to extend beyond one artist or one release cycle.

The company operates across recorded music, production, radio, direct-to-fan tools, licensing readiness, artist submissions, merchandise, press infrastructure and catalog documentation.

THE FOUNDER

Mark Allen

Mark Allen is HLR’s founder, CEO, flagship recording artist and producer. His artist story has its own dedicated profile so the company and the artist remain connected without being confused as the same entity.
