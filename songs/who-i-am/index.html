TRACK 18 · SELF-DEFINITION

Who I Am

The album’s self-definition chapter: after seventeen tracks of labels, memories, arguments and ambition, the narrator gets to say who he is on his own terms.

Identity without permission

B2BAL repeatedly pushes against labels assigned from outside—failure, conflict, diagnosis, history, reputation. Who I Am gathers those threads and puts the authority back with the person living the story.

The song’s place near the end matters. Self-definition lands differently after the listener has already heard the evidence.

Album role

This is the hinge between the personal autobiography and the final outward-facing pair, Truth Bomb and S.O.S..

IdentitySelf-definitionAutonomySurvival

Why Track 18 matters

The album title is a declaration, but this song makes the declaration personal. Before the project turns toward institutions and larger questions, Mark plants a flag in the one territory he can actually claim with certainty: his own identity.
