ABOUT HLR

Built around ownership, development and long-term value.

INDEPENDENT BY DESIGN
Build the infrastructure instead of waiting for permission.

Hustle Legend Records was founded by Mark Allen as an independent home for music, creative development and owned business infrastructure. The goal is not to imitate a major label at smaller scale. It is to build direct systems that let artists and collaborators create, develop and pursue opportunities with clear rights and documented ownership.

Creative control

Music and identity are developed without forcing every artist into one formula.

Rights clarity

Masters, publishing, licensing and collaborator rights are documented instead of assumed.

Long-term infrastructure

Catalog, audience, radio, submissions, merch and business relationships are treated as durable assets.
