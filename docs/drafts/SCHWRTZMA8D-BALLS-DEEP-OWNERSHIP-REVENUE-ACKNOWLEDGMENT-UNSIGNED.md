## 8. Refunds, chargebacks and responsibility for losses
Remedy nondelivery, corrupt files, duplicate charges and materially misdescribed licenses through correction, redelivery or required refund. No blanket “no refunds” rule overrides law or processor rights.
Producer as verified seller processes customer remedies promptly. Internal allocation: ordinary properly authorized seller refunds and chargebacks reverse the affected sale; Producer bears ordinary unavoidable transaction fees unless an HLR-caused fault produced the loss. Producer bears losses caused by his rights/disclosure/product breaches; HLR bears losses caused by its unauthorized sale, payment-routing or security/delivery breach. Shared fault is allocated proportionately. No party bears the other's unrelated debts.
A refund reversal is capped at the customer principal actually refunded, plus documented unrecovered fees properly allocated above. No duplicate recovery is permitted.
Any disputed recovery requires written transaction-level evidence and a reasonable opportunity to respond. HLR may suspend the affected listing/access while investigating but may not seize unrelated funds, cross-collateralize other catalogs or use customer credentials. Only an actual processor hold or separately agreed, quantified and time-limited reserve may delay funds; undisputed amounts remain payable. Unresolved allocation disputes follow Section 12.

## 9. Claims, indemnity and reasonable liability limits — proposed for counsel

No indemnity applies to the indemnified party's own share of fault.
Prompt notice is required; delayed notice reduces obligations only to the extent it materially prejudices defense. The responsible party may control defense with competent counsel reasonably acceptable to the other; neither may settle by admitting fault, transferring rights or imposing unpaid/nonmonetary duties on the other without consent. No speculative fees or unilateral blank-check demand is permitted.
For ordinary contractual service failures, each party's aggregate direct-damage liability is proposed to be capped at the greater of $1,000 or gross BALLS DEEP license revenue during the preceding 12 months. This cap is a new proposal requiring both parties' express agreement.
The cap and proposed mutual exclusion of indirect/consequential damages do not limit fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence, infringement/rights-warranty claims and related indemnities, misappropriated funds, payment/refund/accounting obligations, confidentiality/data-security breaches, or liabilities that law cannot limit.
