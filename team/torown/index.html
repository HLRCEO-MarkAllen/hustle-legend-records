TOROWN

Artist Relations Manager

An artist-first bridge between talent and the label.

Torown serves as Artist Relations Manager for Hustle Legend Records, supporting artist communication, collaboration and the human side of building independent music. His perspective comes from the work itself: writing, recording, releasing and performing as an independent artist.

His connection with Mark Allen and Hustle Legend Records developed through the independent music community before becoming a completed collaboration on “Told U” from the 20-track album Born 2 Be A Legend.
