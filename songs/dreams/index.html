TRACK 17 · FUTURE OWNERSHIP

Dreams

The dream gets practical: peace, free will, ownership, trusted collaborators and control of the creative environment.

Dreaming with receipts

By this point, success is no longer just being heard. The B2BAL era is about building a structure around the art so the future cannot be separated from ownership.

Verified lyric annotations

“fuck that money I prefer my peace”

The dream is deliberately redefined. Money is no longer the highest-value outcome; peace becomes the luxury.

“fuck an oversight I own my rights / this whole damn label down to the mic”

“Down to the mic” makes ownership physical. Masters, publishing and label control stop being abstract paperwork and become control of the entire creative room.

“Nick and Danie when we had this dream / Just look at us, that dreams achieved”

The song folds the HLR team into the payoff. The dream is not solitary—it becomes evidence of a shared plan becoming real.

PeaceOwnershipHLRNickDanie

Why it belongs late

After survival and conflict, the album finally has room to look forward. Dreams sounds different because the narrator now believes the future can be owned instead of merely hoped for.
