TRACK 18 · SELF-DEFINITION

Who I Am

After seventeen tracks of labels, memories, arguments and ambition, the narrator takes back the authority to define himself.

Identity without permission

B2BAL repeatedly pushes against identities assigned from outside—failure, diagnosis, reputation, conflict and history. Who I Am gathers those threads and puts the final authority back with the person living the story.

Verified lyric annotations

“When people need a label just to tell me who I am”

The word “label” works socially and, in the context of an independent artist, carries an industry echo too. The song rejects both kinds of outside ownership: people naming the person and institutions naming the artist.

“ADHD, like they really know me”

The diagnosis may describe part of the experience, but the bar rejects the idea that one description can become a complete identity.

IdentityLabelsADHDAutonomy

Why Track 18 matters

The placement is crucial. Before the album turns outward toward Truth Bomb and S.O.S., Mark plants a flag in the one territory he can claim with certainty: his own identity.
