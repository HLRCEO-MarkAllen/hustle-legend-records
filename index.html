This Was Never Just An Album.

Born 2 Be A Legend is Mark Allen turning years of setbacks, unfinished chapters, family, survival, ambition and raw independence into one project. The record reconnects the artist who disappeared for five years with the man who came back determined to own the work, build the label and leave something behind.

Disappear for five years. Come back with twenty reasons they should've remembered the name.

From personal records like “Karma” to the return statement of “5 Years Later…” and the defiance running through songs like “Rowdy,” the album is built around one idea: legacy is not something somebody gives you. You build it.
