05 · ANESTHESIA

Blacked Out Memories

A darker chapter where drinking stops sounding like celebration and starts sounding like an attempt not to feel.

Behind the song

The track connects memory, Forest Creek and alcohol. The larger idea is not partying for mythology; it is using chaos to outrun pain until forgetting becomes both the goal and the damage.

Verified lyric annotations

“Lost my identity / Drinking became my remedy”

This is the center of the song. “Remedy” usually suggests healing, but here the word is intentionally poisoned: the supposed cure is also part of the destruction.

“The dark days of forest creek / Feeling dead then numbed with drinks”

Forest Creek becomes more than a location. It turns into shorthand for a whole emotional era. “Feeling dead” and then becoming “numbed” suggests the alcohol is not creating happiness—it is muting pain that is already there.

Why it matters

The title captures the contradiction: memories can disappear while consequences remain. In the wider B2BAL story, the loudest rooms can hide the quietest collapse.
