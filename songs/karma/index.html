02 · CONSEQUENCE

Karma

What sounds like a relationship record becomes a family record almost immediately.

The opening changes everything

The song opens with Mark Allen's daughter saying, “Don't go!” That detail reframes the record before the verses even settle in. Separation is no longer only between two adults; there is a child hearing and feeling the consequences too.

That is the emotional engine of the track. The title may suggest payback, but the deeper subject is consequence: what people do, what follows, and who ends up carrying pieces of it.

Why it matters in the album

Placed immediately after the comeback statement of “5 Years Later…,” “Karma” removes the armor. The album has barely announced the return before it shows the personal cost underneath that return.

That tension—confidence beside vulnerability—becomes one of the defining characteristics of Born 2 Be A Legend.
