OFFICIAL BIO

Built from interruption, rebuilt through ownership.

Mark Allen is an independent hip-hop artist, songwriter and producer born and raised in South New Jersey and now based in Breese, Illinois. His music leans into punchlines, layered writing and autobiographical detail without separating the polished record from the harder parts of the story behind it.

Before the current HLR era, Allen recorded under earlier identities including Prodigal Son. A five-year interruption broke the momentum, scattered parts of the old catalog and eventually became material for the comeback itself. Rather than restart as a disposable single campaign, he built Hustle Legend Records around the idea that the music, history, masters and long-term audience should remain connected.

Born 2 Be A Legend, released April 5, 2026, became the first official release of that rebuilt structure: 20 tracks centered on survival, fatherhood, conflict, loyalty, addiction, identity, humor, ambition and creative control.
