TRACK 08 · MEMORY LOOP

Deja Vu

Repeated loss becomes structure: every new goodbye can wake up an older one.

Why the title fits

B2BAL is structured through memory rather than a perfectly straight timeline. Deja Vu makes that structure explicit by treating grief and separation like recurring patterns instead of isolated events.

The title is psychological as much as visual: the present feels familiar because an old wound has been reactivated.

Verified lyric annotations

“same time you passed I died inside”

The line makes grief reciprocal. One person literally dies; part of the narrator's identity dies with them.

“cause losing people that I love feels like Deja Vu”

This is the thesis of the title. Repetition is not supernatural—it is emotional pattern recognition. New loss arrives carrying echoes of older loss.

GriefMemoryLossPatterns

Visual language

Cracked reflections and timepieces fit the song because both imply recurrence: seeing yourself through damage and watching time move forward while grief keeps looping backward.
