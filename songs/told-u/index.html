15 · CONNECTION COMPLETED · LEGEND FILE 015

Told U

Featuring Torown, this song is one of the album's clearest examples of an unfinished idea surviving long enough to become history.

Years in the making

The connection began before Born 2 Be A Legend, when Nick Nasty wanted Mark Allen and Torown to collaborate. The feature did not happen then. Life interrupted the plan, Mark disappeared from music for years, and the idea remained unfinished.

After the comeback and the formation of Hustle Legend Records, Torown was contacted again. This time the record happened—and Mark has described the finished collaboration as exceeding what they had originally imagined.

Why it matters

“Told U” is not just a feature slot. It proves one of the album's recurring ideas: absence does not always erase a connection. Sometimes the people and ideas waiting on the other side of the gap become part of the comeback itself.
