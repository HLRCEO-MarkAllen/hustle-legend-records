LISTENER BEHAVIOR

Different records are doing different jobs.

Acquisition

All Alone was a sudden-discovery signal in the earlier snapshot. The job is not to force another identical play; it is to move that listener deeper into the catalog.

Conversion

Deja Vu combines meaningful reach with the strongest visible save count among the tracks in that earlier snapshot.

Retention

Karma is lower-volume in that earlier window but shows deeper repeat behavior relative to its listener count. It works as a relationship record, not merely a discovery record.

THE POINT

Discovery is the entrance. Intent is the asset.

Hustle Legend Records is not trying to manufacture a fake overnight moment. The goal is to turn real discovery into real curiosity: another song, a save, a follow, a return visit, a deeper understanding of the record. Survival creates the next chapter. The catalog keeps the chapter alive.
