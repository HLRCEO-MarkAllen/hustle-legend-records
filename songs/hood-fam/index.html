04 · CHOSEN FAMILY

Hood Fam

Featuring Alina, the song treats loyalty as something you can build when home has not always felt permanent.

The idea

“Hood Fam” sits in the album where belonging becomes the subject. The larger B2BAL story contains instability, changing environments and pressure, so family here is not limited to blood. It is also about the people who prove they are there.

Why it matters

That chosen-family idea helps explain Hustle Legend Records too. The label story is not only business paperwork; it is also a network of people who stayed connected, returned, collaborated and helped turn a comeback into something bigger than a solo release.
