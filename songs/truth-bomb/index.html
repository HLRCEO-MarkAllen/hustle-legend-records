Truth Bomb

Late in the album, the lens widens from autobiography toward institutions, censorship and the stories people are told to accept.

The shift

Most of Born 2 Be A Legend is intensely personal. “Truth Bomb” changes the scale. Instead of focusing only on Mark Allen's own conflicts, the record documents his skepticism toward powerful institutions and his frustration with subjects he believes are too quickly shut down or sanitized.

Why it matters

The song is strongest when understood as a record of perspective rather than a claim that every suspicion is proven fact. Its role in the album is to show how personal distrust can expand into social distrust—and how the same refusal to self-censor that powers the battle records also appears in political and institutional questions.
