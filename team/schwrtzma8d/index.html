SCHWRTZMA8D

Producer · Beat Store Manager · Southern Illinois

Producer · Beat Store Manager

Soundtracks for real life.

SCHWRTZMA8D is a Southern Illinois producer built on real life, hard work, family, and the belief that music should actually mean something.

His sound comes from more than a studio. It comes from long workdays, responsibilities, fatherhood, relationships, lessons from the past, and the drive to build a better future. Every beat carries a piece of that story.

As part of Hustle Legend Records, SCHWRTZMA8D brings his own identity to the movement—combining hip-hop, country rap, Southern influence, rock energy, and hard-hitting production into music made for artists with something real to say.

He is the younger brother of Hustle Legend Records founder and CEO Mark Allen, bringing a family connection to the label while building a production identity that stands on its own.
