The ear behind the connection.

DJ Nick Nasty serves as A&R Director for Hustle Legend Records, bringing a DJ's instinct for sequencing, chemistry and momentum to the label's creative decisions.

His strongest lane is connecting the right artists to the right records: identifying feature opportunities, building cohesive mixtapes, evaluating remix ideas and helping shape collaborations that make every voice matter.
